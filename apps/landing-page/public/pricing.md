# Pricing — Open Design

Open Design is **free and source-available for local use**. The desktop app
(macOS, Windows, Linux) uses the Open Design Community License 1.0 and BYOK.
You pay only for your own agent/model API usage, which bills directly to your
own provider account. Nothing is proxied through Open Design.

Optional paid tiers add a **managed** experience: bundled model usage credits
(so you do not need your own key) plus hosted cloud deploys. They are optional —
not required to use Open Design.

## Free — source-available desktop app
- Price: **$0 / month for local use**
- License: Open Design Community License 1.0 (full source and terms at
  https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)
- Model access: **BYOK** — bring your own provider key; API usage bills to your
  own account, never proxied through Open Design
- Runs: local-first on your own machine (macOS, Windows, Linux)
- Includes: the full skill + design-system library, every supported coding agent
  (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, OpenCode, Qwen, and more), and artifacts
  saved as files in your own repository
- Get it: https://open-design.ai/download/

## Plus — managed
- Price: **$20 / month** (or $168 / year, 30% off)
- Included model usage credits: $20 / month
- Cloud deploys: up to 3 / month
- For: individuals who prefer bundled credits over BYOK

## Pro — managed (recommended)
- Price: **$100 / month** (or $720 / year, 40% off)
- Included model usage credits: $100 / month
- Cloud deploys: up to 20 / month
- For: professionals shipping regularly

## Max — managed
- Price: **$200 / month** (or $1,176 / year, 51% off)
- Included model usage credits: $200 / month
- Cloud deploys: up to 50 / month
- For: heavy individual users

## Notes
- On the official service, an active Team plan may be required before an
  invitation that would add a second person can be sent or accepted.
- A third party needs commercial authorization to operate Open Design for any
  external customer, embed it in another product or service, or offer it through
  OEM, resale, sublicensing, or white labeling.
- The paid tiers are optional managed services (bundled model credits, hosted
  cloud deploys, or Team features), not a requirement for local software use.
- A personal paid tier or Token/Credit balance is not a Team entitlement by
  itself. Team access must be expressly included in the applicable Team plan,
  order, or account record. A zero balance may pause metered model calls but does
  not by itself cancel an otherwise valid Team entitlement.
- Overage cloud deploys beyond a tier's monthly limit: $2 each.
- Intro/promotional pricing and current annual discounts may differ — see the
  live pricing page for the current numbers: https://open-design.ai/pricing/
