# Open Design brand notice requirements

This file defines the attribution required by condition 1(b) of the
[Open Design Community License 1.0](LICENSE). It defines the Required Brand
Notice for releases that ship with this version of the file.

## Product shell

Any product that uses the Covered Frontend must display the Open Design full
wordmark in its primary product shell:

- Use [`apps/web/public/logo-03.svg`](apps/web/public/logo-03.svg), or an exact
  vector-equivalent export of that wordmark.
- Display it at no less than 120 CSS pixels wide and 20 CSS pixels high on a
  standard-density screen. Proportional scaling for higher-density screens is
  allowed.
- Keep it visible, legible, and unobscured on the primary home or workspace
  surface without requiring the user to open a menu, About dialog, or legal
  page.
- Do not crop, distort, animate into a different mark, or alter the wording or
  geometry. A single-color contrast adjustment is allowed solely to keep the
  mark legible against the surrounding background.

Your own product name and branding may be more prominent. The Open Design mark
must remain a distinct attribution and must not be presented as your product's
primary brand.

## About or Legal surface

An accessible About or Legal surface must include the following text, with a
working link to the Open Design repository and to the `LICENSE` file bundled
with that product or to an immutable copy of that exact license version:

> Powered by [Open Design](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)<br>
> © 2026 Powerformer, Inc. and Open Design contributors<br>
> Licensed under the Open Design Community License 1.0 (see the bundled `LICENSE`).<br>
> This product is independently developed and is not an official, sponsored,
> or endorsed Open Design or nexu-io product.

You may translate the independence statement for the product's locale, but its
meaning must not change. The product shell wordmark and the names “Open Design”
and “nexu-io” must not be translated.

## Limited brand permission

Condition 1(b) of the license permits use, reproduction, and display of the Brand
Assets only as needed to satisfy these requirements. It does not permit use of
Open Design or nexu-io as a product name, company name, domain name,
application icon, or primary brand, and it does not imply endorsement.

A separately written frontend that uses the Covered Software only through an
API and incorporates no material from `apps/web/` is outside the Covered
Frontend definition and does not have to display this notice.

Questions about an implementation or a written branding exception can be sent
to <support@open-design.ai>.
